# First-customer acquisition without advertising spend

Status: **non-transactional prelaunch**. Begin with free educational content and opt-in feedback, not claims that products are currently purchasable.

## Who to reach
Independent residential cleaning owners doing their own quotes; especially solo operators hiring a first teammate, or small teams offering deep / move-out / recurring cleans.

## Core offer
**Free:** cleaning pricing calculator, exportable estimate CSV, printable 10-point pricing checklist and three guides.  
**Paid after launch approval:** Cleaning Quote & Profit Kit, proposed $49 one-time.

## Measurable hypotheses
- Cleaning owners want to see whether labor, supplies, drive time and overhead have been accounted for.
- Downloading a checklist after using the calculator could indicate interest in a richer toolkit.
- Business owners are more likely to pay for cost tracking + reusable scopes + repeat-service comparisons than for another basic quote template.

All three are unvalidated until responses or sales are collected.

## First 10 customer conversations (permission-based)
Seek genuine feedback in online groups that permit business-resource posts, existing cleaning industry contacts and one-to-one conversations initiated with consent. Do not scrape or mass-message groups, promise income, pretend to be a satisfied customer, or post affiliate links without disclosure.

Ask three questions: (1) How do you price weekly, deep and move-out visits today? (2) What part takes the most time or creates errors? (3) Would you pay $49 for a worksheet and editable client documents that solve that problem? Why or why not?

Record answers without storing names, emails or identifiers unnecessarily.

## Two useful honest posts

**Educational:** "Cleaning business owners: one easy way to underprice is to calculate pay rates but leave out travel, supplies and overhead. I put together a free quote calculator and 10-point costing checklist so you can pressure-test your own assumptions. No signup required. If group rules allow resources, here's the link: https://regal-caramel-74ca51.netlify.app/"

**Research:** "I am developing an Excel quote-and-profit workbook and editable estimate templates for small residential cleaning businesses. I'm collecting feedback, not selling yet. When you prepare a weekly or deep-clean quote, is labor time, price competition, or defining scope your biggest problem?"

Check each community's self-promotion rules before posting.

## Lean measures
Track date, platform, post/topic, reach (when legitimately available), link visits (if privacy-aware analytics is enabled later), genuine replies, repeat requests, products wishlisted (if feature is configured), orders after checkout is live, refunds, and earnings after fees/expenses. A post impression or click is not a sale.

## Evidence-based checkpoints
**After 10 real interviews:** identify the strongest painful use case, update product copy only if supported.
**After 3 real orders:** confirm the package is valued and delivered; ask for voluntary honest feedback (never fabricate testimonials).
**After 25 real orders:** analyze revenue, real fee statements, refunds, customer requests, and acquisition sources before evaluating any spending or new business.

No advertising, subscriptions or customer-contact uploads without further authorization. No guarantees on timing or earnings.
