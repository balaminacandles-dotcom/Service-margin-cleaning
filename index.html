THE DETAILS

Quick answers.

Is this suitable for a brand-new cleaning business?

It is designed for solo residential cleaners and small crews familiar with editing spreadsheets. You must enter your own real costs; example numbers are not local market guidance.

Can I use it for recurring clients?

Yes. The recurring sheet lets you model weekly, biweekly and four-weekly pricing and the effect of discounts. It does not schedule visits or process payments.

Will it guarantee more income?

No. It helps organize assumptions and track results. Profit and sales depend on your market, cost structure, execution and actual demand.
